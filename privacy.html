How information submitted through this educational website is handled.

When you send an inquiry, the website collects the information you provide, such as your name, email address, organization, inquiry type, and message.

The inquiry form may use a third-party form-delivery service to send messages to the organization’s email inbox. Please avoid including confidential or sensitive information in your message.
