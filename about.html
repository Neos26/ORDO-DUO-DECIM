About
Our Organization

Ordo Duo Decim means “Order Twelve”—a student Event Management organization established in 2025.

ESTABLISHED 2025

Our
Story

Ordo Duo Decim was formed through our shared Event Management subject, bringing together passionate students with one vision.

We are dreamers and doers, learners and leaders. Though newly established in 2025, we carry the drive to turn every lesson into action and every idea into something real and meaningful.

We plan, organize, and manage events through teamwork, creativity, and effective leadership. We support one another, celebrate every win, and grow through every challenge because we believe great things happen when we work as one.

Every event becomes an opportunity to practice, improve, serve, and create an experience that is prepared with care and meaningful for everyone involved.

01

VISION

To inspire BPEd students and the Metro Angeles Community through meaningful events that promote creativity, leadership, innovation, and positive change.

02

MISSION

To provide BPEd students meaningful opportunities in event planning and organization through experiential learning, collaboration, and professional development—strengthening leadership, creativity, and innovation for positive academic, professional, and community impact.
